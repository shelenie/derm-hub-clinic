Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This translation is provided for convenience. If there’s any discrepancy, the Ukrainian version applies.

This policy explains what data the Derm Hub clinic website (37 Shevchenka St, Boryspil) collects, why it is needed and how we protect it. We process personal data in accordance with the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”.

1. What data we receive

Only what you enter in the booking form yourself:

  • your name — so we know how to address you;
  • your phone number — to confirm the booking and remind you of the visit;
  • the chosen service, doctor, date and time.

Please do not enter diagnoses, test results or other health information in the form. The doctor will discuss them with you in person at the consultation.

2. Why we use it

  • to book you with a doctor and confirm the booking;
  • to send you a reminder before the visit;
  • to contact you if the appointment time changes.

We do not send marketing messages without your separate consent and we do not sell data to third parties.

3. Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box in the booking form. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

4. Who has access to the data

The clinic’s front desk staff and doctors — to the extent needed for booking and the appointment. We may use Telegram or SMS for confirmations and reminders: in that case the delivery service receives only your number and the message text.

5. How long we keep the data

As long as needed for booking and contacting you, or until you withdraw your consent. Medical records kept by the doctor during the appointment are stored according to the rules set by Ukrainian law.

6. How we protect the data

Only staff who need it for their work have access to bookings. We do not make your data publicly available.

7. Cookies

The website does not use advertising cookies and does not track you on other websites. Technical browser data may be stored only for the page to work correctly.

8. Your rights

You can find out what data of yours we store, correct it, ask us to delete it or withdraw your consent to processing. To do so, contact the clinic:

  • phone: +380 68 606 52 69;
  • Telegram: @Doctors_AppointmentDERM_HUB;
  • address: 37 Shevchenka St, Boryspil, daily, 10 AM–8 PM.

If you believe your rights have been violated, you can contact the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

9. Changes to the policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always available on this website; the update date is shown at the top.
